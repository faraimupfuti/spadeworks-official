The 8 Chores Your Bot Does
So You Don't Have To

Whenever a computer can do a repetitive typing chore faster and without making mistakes, let the computer do it.

01 · PAYABLES

Reading & Checking Supplier Bills

When bills arrive in your email or on paper, someone has to read them, type the numbers into your accounting system, and check if the order was really delivered.

🤖 What the bot does: It reads the PDF or email, matches it with your purchase order, types the numbers in 2 seconds, and asks your manager: "Ready to pay?"
02 · RECEIVABLES

Sending Invoices & Chasing Late Money

You did the work, but customers forget to pay you. Your team spends hours sending email reminders and calling people for money.

🤖 What the bot does: Automatically sends polite reminder statements when an invoice is 3 days, 7 days, or 14 days overdue. Money comes in faster.
03 · RECONCILIATION

Bank Reconciliation (No More Excel Headaches)

Comparing your bank statement against your accounting ledger line by line is the slowest, most tiring chore in every business.

🤖 What the bot does: Compares 1,000 bank lines against your receipts in 3 seconds. It matches exact dollars and flags any strange charge for you to review.
04 · PAYROLL & JOURNALS

Salary Math & Journal Postings

Every month, salaries, taxes, pensions, and medical deductions must be calculated and typed into the general ledger.

🤖 What the bot does: Verifies the math, formats the salary journal entries, and stages them neatly for your financial controller's approval.
05 · MONTH-END CLOSE

Drafting Your Month-End Close Pack

Closing the books at the end of the month usually takes 10 to 18 days of late-night work for accounting teams.

🤖 What the bot does: Does the prep work continuously every night. When the last day of the month arrives, the draft close pack is already ready on your desk.
06 · TAX & COMPLIANCE

Gathering Tax Schedules (ZIMRA / VAT / Withholding)

Gathering numbers for tax filings is stressful when spreadsheets have broken formulas or missing dates.

🤖 What the bot does: Pulls together the exact figures and schedules needed for your statutory filings in minutes.
07 · REPORTING

Fresh Reports in the Boss's Inbox

Directors and business owners need to know cash numbers every Monday morning, but accountants are too busy typing data to prepare graphs.

🤖 What the bot does: Automatically generates clean, simple PDF summaries and emails them to leadership on schedule.
08 · DATA CLEANUP

Fixing Messy Spreadsheets

Moving data between an old system and a new system or fixing corrupted Excel sheets by hand takes forever and causes tears.

🤖 What the bot does: Cleans thousands of messy rows, strips bad formatting, and moves numbers safely between systems.
